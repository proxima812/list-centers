The tatarverse catalog holds 399 centers in Russian, and 366 of them are translated into English - 33 cards exist so far only in the Russian version.

The Russian text is the source: every new center appears first in the Russian part of the data, and only later, when the time and the person are found, in English translation. A translation is not required when a center is added, but it is required if you want a full English-language version of the page.

When a center has no translation, it simply is not listed in the English catalog - rather than a direct link to the Russian text sitting under an English address. That is the more honest arrangement: a page is either translated in full, or it is not there yet.

You can help with translation without GitHub - write to the [@the_tatarverse](https://t.me/the_tatarverse) Telegram channel and say which center to translate, or what to fix in a card that is already translated, and the translation will be added.
