Center cards in the catalog now carry a bookmark - the icon in the corner of the card that saves a center to your personal list. You can open the list on the ["Saved"](/en/saved) page.

The list is kept right in the browser, with no account and no server: clear the site data, or open tatarverse in another browser or on another device, and the list will be empty. The limit is 500 saved centers.

In the catalog the bookmark toggles: press it to add, press it again to remove. On the "Saved" page itself each card has a "Delete" button instead of a toggle - everything there is saved already. There is also a "Clear the list" button that removes every card at once, with a confirmation.

The counter and the list stay in sync between tabs of the same browser: add a bookmark in one tab and it appears at once in another one open on /saved.

An empty list shows a hint: press the bookmark in the corner of a card in the catalog, and the center will appear here.
