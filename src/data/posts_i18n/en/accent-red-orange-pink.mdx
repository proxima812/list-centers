Three more accents join the original three: *Утлы Кызыл* (red), *Җылы Наранҗы* (orange), and *Иркә Алсу* (pink). Together with the earlier *Җаным Яшел* (green, the default), *Кадерле Зәнгәр* (blue), and *Яна Шәмәха* (violet), the appearance panel now offers six palettes.

**The radius is part of the accent too.** Every color carries its own corner geometry: red and blue got noticeably tighter corners, violet and pink sit in the middle, green and orange keep the default values. The six colors fall into three pairs by shape - cards, buttons, and fields change not only in hue but in outline.

Round elements - toggles, avatars - are exempt from this rule and hold a fixed shape whatever palette you pick. The print version of the catalog behaves the same way.

You can switch the accent in the same place as the theme and the animations: the appearance panel in the site header.
