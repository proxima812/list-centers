The catalog of centers can now be carried away as a file. On the centers page there is a link, ["Print and PDF list"](/centers/print), that leads to a separate version of the catalog laid out for a sheet of paper rather than a screen.

How it works:

1. Tick the countries you need - several at once, or leave "All countries" and print the whole catalog.
2. Press "Open the print dialog".
3. In the browser dialog pick a printer - or choose "Save as PDF" to get a file on your device.

The list is grouped by country and sorted by city. Each center shows its city, region, category, and the address of its card on the site, so you can always come back to the contacts and the sources.

The print page is set black on white, with no header, no footer, no themes, and no animation: on paper and in a PDF they are of no use, and they cost toner.
