We have updated the information about the centers in the tatarverse catalog. The cards now go into more detail: more context about what the centers do, the directions they work in, their cities, and their communities.

Wherever we managed to find and verify the data, we added sources, contacts, official pages, and other useful links. That makes it quicker to understand what a center does, how to reach it, and where to look for current news.

The catalog keeps growing. If you have spotted an inaccuracy, or you know of a center that is not on the site yet, write to us - we will check the information and add it to the database.
