The tatarverse header now has an appearance panel - the button with a colored circle next to the menu. Every setting for how the site looks lives under it.

**Theme.** Three options: light, dark, and system. The system option follows your device settings and switches along with it, so the site goes dark by itself in the evening.

**Color palette.** The accent color - the one that highlights active filters, the focus ring, and the emphasized word on the home page - switches between three sets: *Җаным Яшел*, *Кадерле Зәнгәр*, and *Яна Шәмәха*. The light and dark versions of each palette are picked separately: one and the same hue cannot read the same way on a white sheet and on near-black.

**Animation can be removed.** Next to them sits a toggle that turns off motion entirely: the home page heading, the catalog counters, the marquee, and every transition on the buttons. Colors, gradients, and layout stay where they are - only the movement goes. If your system has "Reduce motion" enabled, the site respects that without the toggle.

Your choice of theme, palette, and animation is remembered by the browser and applied before the page first paints, so moving between sections never flashes a light background.
