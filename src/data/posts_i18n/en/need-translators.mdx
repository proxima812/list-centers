tatarverse currently has two languages. Russian is the primary one: every post is written in it and all the information about the centers lives there. English is partial - 366 center cards out of 399 have been translated, while the posts and the remaining pages do not appear in English at all yet.

We want to add four more languages: Tatar, Kazakh, Bashkir, and Crimean Tatar. For that we need people who know the language and are willing to translate the texts of the site: the posts, the descriptions of the centers, the interface of the catalog.

**There are no separate sections for these languages in the site's code yet** - no addresses like `/tt/`, `/kk/`, `/ba/`, `/crh/`, and no translation folders of the kind English has (`src/data/centers_i18n/en`). Most likely the translation will be arranged the same way English is: one folder per language, files with the same names as the Russian originals, so that a card in any language leads to one and the same center. But before setting that structure up in the code, we need translators - creating empty folders without them makes no sense.

The first step is people, not code. To gather those who are ready to translate even a little, and to decide together where to begin: with the posts, with the descriptions of the centers, or with the interface of the catalog.

If you know Tatar, Kazakh, Bashkir, or Crimean Tatar and are willing to help, write to the Telegram channel [Бәйсез](https://t.me/the_tatarverse). You do not have to translate the whole site at once: one post, one center, or simply a willingness to answer questions about wording while someone else does the writing is enough.
