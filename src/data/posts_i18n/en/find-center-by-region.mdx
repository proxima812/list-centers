The filters in the catalog are built as a tree rather than a list: first you pick the scope - Russia, abroad, or online - and from there the catalog opens the next level for you.

For Russia the next step is the federal district; for the rest of the world it is the part of the world. Then the country, then the region, then the city. Pick "Perm Krai", for instance, and Perm, Lysva, and Berezniki appear.

The category - Tatar, Tatar-Bashkir, Bashkir, Crimean Tatar - works independently of geography and is always visible, so you can switch it on at any step.

Every filter is computed instantly, with no page reload: the counters next to each item are recalculated at once, and the order of the items does not change, so you never have to hunt for the row that was under your cursor a moment ago.

There is search as well - the field above the catalog, with live suggestions, if you know the name of the center or the city.

The page address keeps your selected filters: copy the link and send it, and whoever follows it will open the same set.

If you need the list on paper or as a PDF, there is a separate [print version of the catalog](/centers/print).
