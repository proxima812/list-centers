Right now, from 31 August to 6 September, Kyrgyzstan is hosting the [VI World Nomad Games](https://worldnomadgames.org/en/nomad2026/). The opening and closing ceremonies are in Bishkek at the Azattyk Arena; most of the competitions take place in Cholpon-Ata, on the northern shore of Lake Issyk-Kul. More than a hundred countries, around three thousand athletes, 43 ethnosport disciplines: kok-boru, er-enish, salbuurun with golden eagles, horse races, belt wrestling. Plus an ethno-village where the country's regions show crafts, music and cuisine, and a scientific conference on ethnosport.

The Games are coming home: the first five editions were hosted by Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan, and after the V Games Kazakhstan handed the baton back.

And here is what matters if you have travelled there: **about 21,400 Tatars and some thousand Bashkirs live in Kyrgyzstan**, and they have their own organizations. Our catalog now holds **17 of them** — and nearly all sit exactly where the Games are taking you anyway.

## Bishkek: the opening and the closing

The country's umbrella organization is the **Tatar-Bashkir cultural center "Tugan Tel"**, founded in 1988 and part of the Assembly of the People of Kyrgyzstan. The address is 78 Pushkin St., offices 212–213, inside Chyngyz Aitmatov Park. The center runs a Sunday school of the Tatar language and produces a weekly radio show and a monthly TV show. Its live account is [@tatardiaspora.kg](https://www.instagram.com/tatardiaspora.kg/) — "Tatars and Bashkirs of Kyrgyzstan", almost five thousand followers. It is the only working social media account of a Tatar organization in the country, so that is the place to write.

"Tugan Tel" has organized the Bishkek Sabantuy for more than eighteen years running. This year it took place on 7 June in the Karagach Grove, free admission, with performers from Tatarstan.

Three more organizations work in the capital: **"Berdemlek"** ("Unity"), the **Kyrgyz-Tatar Society of Friendship and Cooperation**, active since 2000, and the **Bashkir cultural center "Kurultai of Bashkirs"** — the only association in the country with a purely Bashkir agenda.

## Cholpon-Ata and Issyk-Kul: where the competitions are

Cholpon-Ata itself has a **Tatar society**, headed by Arslan Gazizovich Yusupov. It is small, has no website or social media, and is known from the directory of the World Congress of Tatars.

In Karakol, on the eastern shore of the lake, there are two: the Tatar-Bashkir association **"Tatulyk"** (listed as "Tatyulyk" in the registry of the World Kurultai of Bashkirs) and the Tatar association **"Duslyk"**. They are different organizations with different registrations, heads and addresses — people confuse them often, so we wrote that out explicitly in both entries.

## Along the road between Bishkek and Issyk-Kul

The highway from the capital to the lake passes through **Kant** and **Tokmok**, and both towns have Tatar societies. The Tokmok one appears in the Tatar Encyclopedia under the name "Idel".

West of Bishkek, in **Kara-Balta**, works the Bashkir-Tatar cultural center "Idel". In the south, in **Osh**, there are the Tatar-Bashkir center **"Ilkaem"** (since 1991) and the Bashkir center of Osh Region **"Ak Tirma"**, the "White Yurt". There are also centers in **Jalal-Abad** ("Idel"), **Naryn**, **Talas** and the village of **Belovodskoye**.

## Being honest about what we do not know

Of the seventeen centers, exactly one has social media. The rest exist in registries — the Coordination Council of Russian Compatriots, the World Congress of Tatars, the World Kurultai of Bashkirs — but not on the internet. For some we only have the name of a director and a town.

That does not mean they are gone. A small organization in Naryn or Talas may gather people for Sabantuy every year and still have no page and no account. It is simply almost impossible to find from another country.
