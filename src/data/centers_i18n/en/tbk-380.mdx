## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Orenburg Oblast is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan". The center was created as part of the decision to establish Bashkir historical and cultural centers in regions where Bashkirs live compactly, and works to preserve the Bashkir language, traditions and culture in the Orenburg area.
