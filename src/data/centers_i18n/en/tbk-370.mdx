## About

The National Center of Crimean Tatars was established in 1976 in the USA and brings together the politically active part of the Crimean Tatar diaspora. Alongside the American Association of Crimean Turks (founded in 1961), the center takes part in the civic, cultural and human rights work of the Crimean Tatar community in the United States.

Crimean Tatars in the USA live mainly in New York (neighborhoods of Brooklyn and Queens), Chicago and Detroit. The first community center for cultural events operated in the Brooklyn neighborhood of Flatbush and changed address several times between 1961 and 1972. Today the community center of the American Association of Crimean Turks is located in Brooklyn at 4509 New Utrecht Avenue.
