## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center "Saitbaba" is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan" in the village of Saitbaba, Gafuriysky District. The center preserves and develops Bashkir traditional culture, language and customs, and holds national festivals and educational events.
