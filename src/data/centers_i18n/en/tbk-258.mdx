## About

"Tugan Tel" is the umbrella Tatar-Bashkir organization of Kyrgyzstan. Founded in 1988, it is part of the Assembly of the People of Kyrgyzstan and marked its 20th anniversary in 2008. The center runs a Sunday school of the Tatar language and produces a weekly radio program and a monthly television program, both called "Tugan Tel". Branches operate in every region of the country: "Ilkaem" in Osh, "Tatulyk" in Karakol, "Idel" in Jalal-Abad, and others in Tokmok, Kara-Balta and elsewhere.

The center organizes the annual Bishkek Sabantuy. In 2026 the festival took place on 7 June in the Karagach Grove with free admission, folk games, traditional contests and performances by artists from Tatarstan. The center's public Instagram account is [@tatardiaspora.kg](https://www.instagram.com/tatardiaspora.kg/); the same handle is listed as its contact in the 2GIS directory and in festival announcements.
