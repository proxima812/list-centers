## About

"Tatarcha Dvizhukha in Armenia" is the gathering point for the Tatars of Yerevan. The group has no formal registration: it is a self-organized community that lives on Telegram and turns online acquaintances into offline meetups, Tatar-language conversation practice and shared celebrations.

The community is listed in the directory of Tatar and Bashkir groups of Europe and the Americas maintained by the curators of diaspora chats, where Armenia is represented by a single entry — the Yerevan channel. As of August 2026 it has 47 subscribers; in scale it is closest to the Georgian chat "Tatars of Tbilisi" and the Hungarian "Tatar Hungary".

Armenia is a country with no historical Tatar community: the Tatar presence here has formed through relocation in recent years, so the diaspora has neither a national-cultural autonomy nor a centre affiliated with the coordinating council of compatriots.
