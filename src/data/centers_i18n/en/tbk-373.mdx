## Details

- A public organization uniting Bashkir national and cultural organizations worldwide
- The first World Qurultay of Bashkirs was held on 1–2 June 1995 in Ufa
- Unites about 128 national and cultural public organizations

## About

The World Qurultay of Bashkirs is the largest public association of the Bashkir people, created to consolidate the nation and preserve its cultural heritage. The first congress took place on 1–2 June 1995 in Ufa with about 800 delegates from Bashkortostan, 28 regions of the Russian Federation and 19 countries. The Qurultay coordinates the work of national and cultural centers and of the executive committees of oblast, district and city Bashkir qurultays across Russia and abroad, and supports the Bashkir diaspora outside the country.
