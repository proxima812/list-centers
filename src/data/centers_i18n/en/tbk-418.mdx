## About

"Tatars and Bashkirs in Slovenia" is a country-wide community bringing together Tatars and Bashkirs living in Ljubljana, Maribor and other cities. As of August 2026 it has 134 members.

The group keeps to a deliberately neutral agenda: its rules state explicitly that discussions stay away from political and religious topics. This format is typical of small European diaspora chats, where members come from different countries and different backgrounds, and what unites them is language and origin.

The community is listed in the directory of Tatar and Bashkir groups of Europe and the Americas, where Slovenia sits next to Serbia; Croatia and the other countries of the former Yugoslavia have no chats of their own yet.
