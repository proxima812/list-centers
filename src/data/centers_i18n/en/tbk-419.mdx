## About

"Baires Tatarcha | Tatars in Argentina / Buenos Aires" is the community of Tatars in the Argentine capital. As of August 2026 it has 253 members, making it the second most significant Tatar association in Latin America after the Peruvian "Tatlama".

The name plays on a local shorthand: porteños call Buenos Aires "Baires", and "tatarcha" means "in Tatar" in the Tatar language. The community grew out of the wave of relocation to Argentina after 2022 and works as a mutual-aid platform: legalization, renting, work, finding fellow countrymen and organizing meetups.

Unlike "Tatlama", which is registered in Peru and claims coverage of all Latin America, the Buenos Aires group remains an informal city community with no legal entity.
