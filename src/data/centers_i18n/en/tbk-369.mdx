## Details

- One of the oldest organizations of the Crimean Tatar diaspora in the USA
- Founded in 1961
- Crimean Tatar name: Qırım Türkleri Amerika Cemiyeti

## About

The American Association of Crimean Turks was founded in 1961 in New York and is one of the oldest and most significant organizations of the Crimean Tatar diaspora in the USA. The association runs educational programs on national history and language for children and young people, holds cultural events, and celebrates national and Muslim holidays. Its first community center was located in the Brooklyn neighborhood of Flatbush; between 1961 and 1972 the center moved several times within the city.
