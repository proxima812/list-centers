## About

"Avdet" is the Tashkent City Cultural Centre of Crimean Tatars, uniting the Crimean Tatar community of Uzbekistan. The name means "return" — it was also the name of the newspaper of the Crimean Tatar national movement. The centre is located at 45 Bobur Avenue, in the same building as the Tatar and Bashkir cultural centres of Tashkent, and runs communities on Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, VKontakte and Odnoklassniki.
