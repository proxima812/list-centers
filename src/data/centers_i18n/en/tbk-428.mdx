- Awarded the World Congress of Tatars medal "For Services to the Tatar People"

## About

The Regional National-Cultural Autonomy of Tatars of Volgograd Oblast is listed in the Volgograd city administration's registry of national public associations, headed by Anver Aminovich Bulatov, a member of the Council of the Federal National-Cultural Autonomy of Tatars, awarded the World Congress of Tatars medal "For Services to the Tatar People."

It is not the same organization as the "Congress of Tatars of Volgograd Oblast" headed by Sayfulina Aisylu Radikovna (different address, different director), nor is it the successor to the Regional National-Cultural Autonomy of Tatars of Volgograd and Volgograd Oblast headed by Rashid Khantimirov, which was liquidated in 2020 — only the registration type and the region's general name coincide.
