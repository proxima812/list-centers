## About

Australian Tatar Forum - Kurultai is a community initiative that brings together Tatars and Bashkirs from different regions of Australia. The forum is organised by an independent Tatar community group together with the Tatar-Bashkir Association of Australia in South Australia. Its organising committee includes members of the Tatar diaspora from South Australia, Western Australia and New South Wales.

The initiative's mission is to strengthen Tatar communities across Australia, help preserve Tatar identity and raise the profile of Tatar culture in the country. Its stated goals include fostering a sense of belonging, passing the language, history, traditions and cultural heritage on to the next generations, and building solidarity and mutual support among Australian Tatars.

The first Australian Tatar Forum Kurultai is scheduled to take place in Adelaide on 26-27 September 2026. The programme includes a conference, an exhibition, a public concert and a family camp.
