# Democratic Union of Turkish-Muslim Tatars of Romania

## Contacts

- Director: Dzhelil Esergep

## About

The Democratic Union of Turkish-Muslim Tatars of Romania (Romanian: UDTTMR) is an ethnic minority organisation representing the Tatar community of Romania, including in parliament — Romania's national minorities have guaranteed representation. It was founded on 29 December 1989 as the Turkish-Muslim Democratic Union of Romania; on 12 April 1990 a faction split off and became the Democratic Turkish Union of Romania.

Most of the community are Crimean Tatars (around 24,000), living mainly in Constanța County (Dobruja). Crimean Tatars began resettling in Ottoman Dobruja after 1783, settling around Medgidia, Babadag, Constanța, Tulcea and Silistra. The union works to preserve the language, culture and religious traditions of the country's Tatar-Turkic population.
