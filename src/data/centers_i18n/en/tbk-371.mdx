## About

The Crimean Turks Culture and Mutual Aid Association (Kırım Türkleri Kültür ve Yardımlaşma Derneği) is the central organization of the Crimean Tatar diaspora in Turkey and the focus of its civic and cultural activity. The association has branches in various parts of the country. Its general center coordinates the aid campaigns run by Crimean Tatar organizations abroad, maintaining close ties with communities in the USA, Germany, the Netherlands, Romania, Bulgaria and Australia.
