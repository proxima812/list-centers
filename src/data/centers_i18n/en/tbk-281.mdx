## About

"TATARS and BASHKIRS OF KYRGYZSTAN" is the public account of the country's Tatar-Bashkir community: meetups, events and Sabantuy announcements.

It is not a separate organization. The same handle is listed as the contact of the Tatar-Bashkir cultural center "Tugan Tel" in the 2GIS directory and in the announcement of the 2026 Bishkek Sabantuy, where "Tugan Tel" is named as organizer with the note "Instagram: @tatardiaspora.kg". The entry is kept separately only because many people know the account under its own name; substantively it belongs to the "Tugan Tel" center.
