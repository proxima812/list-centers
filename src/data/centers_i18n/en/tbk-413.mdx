## Contacts

- Chairperson: Alfiya Badretdinova (Dubai)
- Deputy chairpersons: Shamil and Aysylu Sitdikov

## About

The alliance was founded in April 2023 with the support of the World Congress of Tatars. It covers around seventeen Arab countries — the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Oman, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Yemen, Jordan, Morocco, Tunisia, Lebanon, Iraq and others; by the organization's own estimate, more than 10,000 Tatars live in the region. Chairperson Alfiya Badretdinova has lived in Dubai for over seven years.

The largest community is in the UAE (at least 5,000 Tatars), centred on Dubai; communication runs through WhatsApp and Telegram groups ("TATARS in MENA"), the main group having more than 550 members. In Saudi Arabia over 150 Tatars are connected (the activists are Niyaz and Aliya Ikhsanov), in Egypt the community is represented by Ramil Garipov (Cairo, since 2019), in Tunisia by Rafael Daminov, in Yemen by the physician Svetlana Fazlyeva, and in Kuwait more than 15 Tatars have been identified.

The main activities are the annual Sabantuy in Dubai (which drew 2,500 participants in 2023), iftars during Ramadan and desert picnics.
