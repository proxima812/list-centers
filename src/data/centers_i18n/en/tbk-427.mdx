## About

The public organization "Congress of Tatars of Volgograd Oblast, Preservation of National Culture and Cultural-Spiritual Development of Tatars" is listed in the Volgograd city administration's registry of national public associations, headed by Sayfulina Aisylu Radikovna.

It is not the successor to the Regional National-Cultural Autonomy of Tatars of Volgograd and Volgograd Oblast headed by Rashid Khantimirov: that organization was liquidated by court decision on 6 March 2020. "Congress of Tatars of Volgograd Oblast" is a separate structure with a different leader and a different address.
