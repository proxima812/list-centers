## About

"Yashen" is a Tatar youth public organization in Yekaterinburg and Sverdlovsk Oblast. The name means "lightning" in Tatar; its founders also read it as a shortening of "Yashlar bugen" — "Youth today".

The organization began work on 2 August 2007 and received state registration in May 2010. Its first members met through an online community of Tatars and Bashkirs of the Urals and then joined forces to run cultural, educational and civic events.

"Yashen" helps young people discover the Tatar language, history and culture. Its formats include a conversation club, national holidays, creative and cooking workshops, film screenings, lectures, meetings with public figures and projects to preserve Tatar heritage. The organization also takes part in the work of the Tatar Cultural Centre of Yekaterinburg and cooperates with other Tatar associations in the region.

According to the Permanent Representation of the Republic of Tatarstan, the core group of "Yashen" continued its public work in 2025. Publications about events in 2025-2026 name Rinat Kashtanov as head of the organization.
