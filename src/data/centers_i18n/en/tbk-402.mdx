## About

Alima Academy is an online educational platform with materials on the Tatar language, literature and culture. The site offers lessons and thematic learning resources in Tatar and English.

The project's founder, Alima Salakhutdinova, is connected with the Tatar community of Montreal and represents Alima Academy as one of the Canadian ambassadors of the Australian Tatar Forum - Kurultai.
