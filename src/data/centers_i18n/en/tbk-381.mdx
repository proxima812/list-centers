## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Sverdlovsk Oblast is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan". The center takes part in regional cultural events and forums, including the "Etker" forum, and preserves and develops the Bashkir language, traditions and national culture in the Middle Urals.
