## About

The Tatar public association "Duslyk" was registered on 19 May 2010 (No. 111985-3302-ОО) and unites the Tatar diaspora of Karakol and the Issyk-Kul Region.

Two separate Tatar organizations operate in Karakol: "Duslyk" (this entry, registered in 2010, chaired by Ishmurzina Nailya Yulduzovna) and the Tatar-Bashkir public association "Tatulyk" (registered in 2004, No. 2896-3302-ОО, chaired by Nasibullin Rashid Rakhimovich) — they have different registration numbers, leaders and addresses, and should not be confused.
