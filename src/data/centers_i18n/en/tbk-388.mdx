## Details

- Operates in the Bukhara Region of Uzbekistan
- Preserves the language, customs and traditions of Tatars and Bashkirs

## About

The Tatar-Bashkir Cultural Center of Bukhara Region is one of the ethnocultural associations of Tatars and Bashkirs in Uzbekistan. Together with the Tatar-Bashkir centers of the Kashkadarya and Samarkand regions and the Zaki Validi Bashkir Public and Cultural Center, it preserves and develops the national language, customs and traditions of the Tatar and Bashkir peoples of the country.
