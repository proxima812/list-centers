## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center "Temyasovo" is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan" in the village of Temyasovo, Baymaksky District. The center works to preserve and promote Bashkir history, language and traditional culture and organizes national events. Temyasovo is known as one of the historical capitals of Bashkir autonomy.
