## About

Idel Tatar Community of Western Australia is a community page for the Tatars of Western Australia. The community is based in Perth and run by an independent group of local Tatars. The page also uses the name "Idel Qara Aqqoş".

Marat Gallyam represents the community as one of the Western Australian ambassadors of the Australian Tatar Forum - Kurultai.
