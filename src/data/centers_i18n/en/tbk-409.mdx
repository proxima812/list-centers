## About

Muzułmańska Gmina Wyznaniowa w Warszawie is one of the five communities of the Muslim Religious Union in the Republic of Poland. The Warsaw community was the first to resume activity after the Second World War: its first post-war meeting was held on 29 July 1945, and in 1946 the community was re-established as the Central Muslim Religious Community in Warsaw, with headquarters on Mokotowska Street.

Today the community uses a prayer hall at the Tatar Muslim cemetery at 8 Tatarska Street - the historic mizar of the Warsaw Tatars. Together with the Union of Tatars of the Republic of Poland, the community holds cultural events, including the Tatar spring festival of Nowruz.

The Muslim Religious Union unites Hanafi Sunnis, above all Polish Tatars whose ancestors settled in the lands of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth more than 600 years ago.
