## About

The Bashkir Cultural Center "Kurultai of Bashkirs" of the Kyrgyz Republic is listed in the official registry of Bashkir public organizations worldwide maintained by the International Union of Public Associations "World Kurultai (Congress) of Bashkirs". Its head is Zarema Ravilevna Kazanbaeva, Bishkek.

It is the only organization in the Kyrgyz capital with a purely Bashkir agenda: the country's other associations are Tatar-Bashkir ("Tugan Tel" in Bishkek, "Ilkaem" in Osh, "Tatulyk" in Karakol, "Idel" in Kara-Balta and Jalal-Abad). The Bashkir community of Kyrgyzstan is small — about a thousand people by the estimate of the Coordination Council of Russian Compatriots, compared with some 21,400 Tatars. No website or social media accounts were found.
