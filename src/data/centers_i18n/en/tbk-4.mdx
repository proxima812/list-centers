## About

The American Turko-Tatar Association is a nonprofit organization serving the San Francisco Bay Area Tatar community since 1960. It preserves Tatar culture and traditions and organizes community events, including an annual Sabantuy celebration. The Instagram account `@sf_tatars` is the association's official page rather than a separate organization.
