## About

"TATARS AND BASHKIRS IN SERBIA" is the largest Tatar and Bashkir association in the Balkans: as of August 2026 the chat has 541 members. The community states three functions of its own: conversation, help and events.

Serbia became one of the main destinations for relocation from Russia and Tatarstan after 2022, and the chat grew out of practical mutual aid: paperwork, housing, work and finding halal food. Over time offline meetups and national holidays were added.

The community is listed in the directory of Tatar and Bashkir groups of Europe and the Americas. In numbers the Serbian group outstrips both the Slovenian (134 members) and the Portuguese one (17) — effectively making it the diaspora's anchor point in the region.
