## About

The official title of the post is "Trade and Economic Representative of the Republic of Tatarstan at the Trade Representation of the Russian Federation in the Republic of India". The decision to open a representation in New Delhi was taken following the BRICS summit in Kazan in October 2024 — the reciprocal step was the opening of the Consulate General of India in Kazan.

In May 2025, at the "Russia — Islamic World: KazanForum", Tatarstan's Minister of Industry and Trade Oleg Korobchenko spoke of a timeline of "a month or two"; by 2026 the representation is operating.

India is among the republic's leading foreign economic partners: trade amounted to 353 million dollars in 2025, the country accounts for 10.5% of Tatarstan's agricultural exports, and in the first quarter of 2026 trade turnover doubled.

There is no Tatar diaspora organization in India — the representation remains the only permanent Tatarstan structure in the country.
