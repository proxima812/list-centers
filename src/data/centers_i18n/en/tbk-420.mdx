## About

"Miras" ("heritage") is an association of the Tatars and Bashkirs of Cairo, founded in early October 2024. Ramil Garipov, who moved to Egypt with his family in 2019, was elected chairperson. The association works as a deliberately joint Tatar-Bashkir body: in Garipov's words, these are "one people" with shared traditions and culture.

Its main focus is educational. In its first year the association held workshops on Tatar ornament in Egyptian mosques and its place in Islamic architecture, an excursion to Alexandria, gatherings devoted to national dress and cuisine, and iftars during Ramadan. For children it organized online Tatar lessons with teachers from Tatarstan's gymnasiums, so that the language does not remain purely domestic.

On 26 April 2025, together with the Coordinating Council of Russian Compatriots' Organizations and the Rossotrudnichestvo mission, "Miras" held a Sabantuy at the Russian House in Cairo — the first in the history of the African continent. The history of the festival was explained in three languages: Russian, Tatar and Arabic; the guests included the poet Aygul Nabiullina and the accordion player Bulat Khismatov.

On 24 April 2026 the Sabantuy at the Russian House in Cairo was repeated, this time with performers from the Kazan City Philharmonic, dance workshops, folk contests and ceramic painting.
