## About

The association was formally established in December 2018 as an open framework for the social and cultural life of compatriots from Tatarstan and Bashkortostan. Its members live in all seven emirates - Abu Dhabi, Ajman, Dubai, Ras Al Khaimah, Umm Al Quwain, Fujairah and Sharjah - with Dubai as the main hub of activity.

Its work covers preserving traditional culture through national holidays and customs, fostering patriotic awareness, supporting education and youth participation, developing tourism and business opportunities for Russian speakers, and promoting Russian-Arab dialogue.

Tatar iftars in Dubai have become an annual tradition of the association. A classic Sabantuy is harder to arrange in the Emirates: gathering a large crowd and holding a fair require special permits.

In December 2018 the association signed memoranda of cooperation with the Assembly of the Peoples of Russia and the Eurasian Academy of Television and Radio, covering humanitarian work, cultural exchange and media coverage of diaspora life.
