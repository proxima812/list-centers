## About

Bursa Kırım Türkleri Kültür ve Yardımlaşma Derneği is the Bursa branch of the Association for the Culture and Mutual Aid of Crimean Turks, the central organisation of the Crimean Tatar diaspora in Turkey, headquartered in Ankara. Bursa is one of the historic centres of Tatar and Nogai emigration: settlers from Crimea arrived in the Bursa vilayet throughout 1853-1914.

The branch runs Crimean Tatar language courses, including a free online conversation club ("Kırım Tatarca Konuşma Kulübü") held on Wednesday evenings every two weeks. Each year on 18 May the association holds commemorative events in Bursa marking the 1944 deportation and genocide of the Crimean Tatars. Its newsletter has been published since March 1996.
