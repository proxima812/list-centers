## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Okrug — Yugra is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of the Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan". The center serves the large Bashkir diaspora of the oil and gas regions of Western Siberia, preserves the language, traditions and culture, organizes national festivals and maintains ties with the historical homeland.
