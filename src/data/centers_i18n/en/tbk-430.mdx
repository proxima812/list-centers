## About

"Ak Tirma" ("White Yurt") is the Bashkir public center of the Osh Region, listed in the official registry of Bashkir public organizations worldwide kept by the World Kurultai of Bashkirs. Its head is Milya Sarievna Temirova-Zhamby, Osh.

The center operates in the same city as the Tatar-Bashkir cultural center "Ilkaem" but is recorded separately and with a Bashkir agenda. Its name repeats an image common among Bashkir organizations: the white yurt in which elders gathered to decide important matters. No website or social media accounts were found, and no mentions beyond the Kurultai registry could be located in open sources.
