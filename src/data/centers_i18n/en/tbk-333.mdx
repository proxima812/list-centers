## About

The Kara-Balta Bashkir-Tatar Cultural Center "Idel" brings together the Tatar and Bashkir communities of Kara-Balta and the Jayyl District of Chuy Region. The organization appears in two registries at once: the directory of Tatar organizations abroad kept by the World Congress of Tatars (as "Tatar Society of Kara-Balta") and the registry of Bashkir public organizations worldwide kept by the World Kurultai of Bashkirs, which gives its full name and its head, Rafail Sultanovich Garipov. According to the "Tugan Tel" center it belongs to the country's network of Tatar-Bashkir centers. No website or social media accounts were found.
