## Details

- The first purely Bashkir center in Kazakhstan
- Founded in 1990 in Aktau

## About

The Bashkir Cultural Center in Aktau is the first purely Bashkir national and cultural center in Kazakhstan, founded in 1990. It works to preserve the Bashkir language, culture and traditions among the Bashkir diaspora of Mangystau Region. Along with the "Akbuzat" center in Astana, it is one of the Bashkir associations of Kazakhstan, where Bashkir centers have historically operated together with Tatar ones.
