## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Tuymazinsky District is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan". The center preserves and promotes Bashkir culture, language and traditions and organizes national festivals and educational work in the west of the Republic of Bashkortostan.
