## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Chelyabinsk Oblast is located in the village of Argayash, a historical center of compact Bashkir settlement in the Southern Urals (in the 1930s the Argayash Bashkir national okrug existed here). The center preserves and develops the Bashkir language, traditions and culture and organizes national festivals and educational work.
