## About

Almaniya Tatarca is a grassroots group of Tatars living in Germany. It organises Tatar-language events for children and adults, along with meet-ups and conversation clubs that help participants practise the language and stay connected to Tatar culture.

The group is one of the organisers of the Forum of European Tatars, scheduled for Frankfurt am Main on 25-27 September 2026. Elza Nabiullina represents Almaniya Tatarca as one of the German ambassadors of the Australian Tatar Forum - Kurultai.
