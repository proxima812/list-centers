## About

Bayram Creative Group is a children's creative group in New South Wales. Its members come together to learn, share knowledge and preserve their cultural heritage. The group performs at Tatar community events: at the TurkFest festival in Sydney the children presented two Tatar songs.

Elmira Abuzarova represents Bayram Creative Group as one of the New South Wales ambassadors of the Australian Tatar Forum - Kurultai.
