## About

The Kurultai of Bashkirs in the UAE and Iran is listed in the official registry of Bashkir public organizations worldwide maintained by the International Union of Public Associations "World Kurultai (Congress) of Bashkirs". The organization operates across two countries at once: it has two addresses — Dubai and Tehran — and separate contact numbers in the UAE, Iran and Russia. Its head is Azat Yulaevich Dilmukhametov.

For Iran this is the only recorded Turkic-Tatar diaspora association. The Iranian Tatar community is estimated at around a million people, but they are mainly descendants of the 13th-century Mongol campaigns and of merchants who settled in the north of the country; because of heavy assimilation — in Golestan and Khorasan the so-called Kara-Tatars shifted to Turkmen and Turkish — they have no public organizations of their own. Separately, the Tatar communities of Afghanistan, Pakistan and Iran have plans to create a single Council of Tatars, but that structure remains at the stage of preliminary agreements (see the entry on the Council of Afghan Tatars).
