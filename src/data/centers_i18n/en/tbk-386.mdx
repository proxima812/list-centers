## About

The "Akbuzat" Bashkir Ethno-Cultural Center operates in Astana (Nur-Sultan) and is the second purely Bashkir center in Kazakhstan. It runs Sunday Bashkir language lessons by video link with teachers from Bashkortostan, the yiyin festival, Days of Bashkir Culture, workshops on playing the kurai and kubyz and on archery, as well as youth and summer programs. In July 2019 the center moved to the House of Friendship and joined the Assembly of People of Kazakhstan. At the World Kurultai of Bashkirs in Ufa (2019) it was recognized as the most active Bashkir center abroad.
