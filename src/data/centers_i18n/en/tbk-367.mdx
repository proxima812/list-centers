## About

The Mejlis of the Crimean Tatar People is the single highest representative body of the Crimean Tatar people, elected by the Qurultay. It was formed in 1991. The Mejlis represents the interests of Crimean Tatars and coordinates ties with the Crimean Tatar diaspora in Turkey, Germany, Lithuania, Romania, France, Poland, the USA, Belgium, Uzbekistan, Canada, the Netherlands, Cyprus and mainland Ukraine.
