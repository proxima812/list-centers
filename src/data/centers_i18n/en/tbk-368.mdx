## About

The Qurultay of the Crimean Tatar People is the representative congress, or national parliament, of the Crimean Tatars. The first Qurultay of the modern era was held in 1991; by its decision the Mejlis was formed as the highest representative body of the people between Qurultay sessions. The Qurultay sets the main directions of national self-government and representation of the Crimean Tatar people.
