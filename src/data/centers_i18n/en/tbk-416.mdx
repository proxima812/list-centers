## About

"Tatars and Bashkirs in Portugal" is a country-wide community not tied to any single city: its members are spread between Lisbon, Porto and the Algarve coast, so the chat works as a shared space for the whole country.

The group belongs to the network of chats prefixed `tatbash_`, which diaspora curators run to a common template across European countries — the same directory lists Poland, Latvia, Estonia, Slovakia and Finland alongside it. The Portuguese branch is the youngest and smallest in that network: as of August 2026 it has 17 members.

Its purpose matches that of the neighbouring country chats: helping newcomers with everyday matters, finding fellow countrymen and organizing occasional meetups. The Tatars of Portugal have no separately registered organization, national-cultural autonomy or centre affiliated with the coordinating council of compatriots.
