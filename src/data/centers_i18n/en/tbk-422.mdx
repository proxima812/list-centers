## About

The Plenipotentiary Representation of the Republic of Tatarstan in Turkmenistan was registered on 11 November 2009 and represents the interests of the republic's state authorities in the country. It operates at the Trade Representation of the Russian Federation in Turkmenistan, in Ashgabat.

Ruslan Kharisovich Kabirov has served as plenipotentiary representative since 11 October 2016; before his appointment he was First Deputy Minister of Industry and Trade of Tatarstan. In October 2025 the Rais of Tatarstan extended his term by another three years.

Trade between Tatarstan and Turkmenistan exceeded 39 million dollars in 2024; the largest anchor project is the KAMAZ training and production centre, in whose building the representation is housed.

There are no Tatar national-cultural associations in Turkmenistan: the law on public associations effectively rules out the registration of ethnic organizations, so the representation remains the only permanent Tatarstan structure in the country.
