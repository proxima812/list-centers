## About

The Tatar language school in Karaganda is an active Tatar-Bashkir association in Karaganda Region. According to a representative of the local community, three Tatar-Bashkir centers effectively operate in Karaganda, but it is the Tatar language school that runs on a regular basis.

Since May 2026 the school has belonged to the Congress of Tatars and Bashkirs and works as its Karaganda Region branch. Tatar centers in Temirtau, Aktas, Abay and Shakhtinsk are also mentioned in the region; their contact details require separate confirmation.
