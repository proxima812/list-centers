## About

The Zaki Validi Bashkir Public and Cultural Center was founded in June 1990 to preserve Bashkir national culture, language and traditions. The center brings together six performing groups, among them the Bashkir folklore ensemble "Agidel" and the "Sandugach" ensemble. It is the main cultural institution of the Bashkirs of Uzbekistan (about 23,000 people) and works together with the Tatar-Bashkir centers of the Bukhara, Kashkadarya and Samarkand regions. In 2020 it marked its 30th anniversary. It is located at 45 Bobur Avenue, together with the city's Tatar and Crimean Tatar centers.
