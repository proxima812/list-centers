## About

Muzułmańska Gmina Wyznaniowa w Bohonikach is one of the five communities of the Muslim Religious Union in the Republic of Poland, alongside those of Białystok, Kruszyniany, Gdańsk and Warsaw. It was founded by Lipka Tatars in the 17th century: the privilege granting the villages of Bohoniki and Drahle to the Tatars is dated 12 March 1679.

The community owns a wooden mosque, still used for Friday and holiday prayers, two mizars (Muslim cemeteries), one of which is still in use, and a community house. The mizar in Bohoniki covers two hectares and remains the largest surviving Tatar cemetery in Poland; on 22 October 2012 it was recognised as a historical monument by decree of the President of the Republic of Poland.

Together with Kruszyniany, Bohoniki forms the Tatar ethnographic "Podlasie pair" - two villages where the unbroken tradition of the Polish Tatars has been preserved since the 17th century.
