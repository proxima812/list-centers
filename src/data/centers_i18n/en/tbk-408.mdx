## About

Muzułmańska Gmina Wyznaniowa w Kruszynianach belongs to the Muslim Religious Union in the Republic of Poland. On 12 March 1679 King John III Sobieski granted the Tatars the village of Kruszyniany along with the neighbouring villages of Nietupa, Łużany and part of Poniatowicze. The Lipka Muslims who settled here had fought for the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth; among those who settled in Kruszyniany, with some 45 families, was Colonel Samuel Murza Krzeczowski, who saved the king's life at the Battle of Párkány in 1683.

The wooden mosque in Kruszyniany is the oldest surviving Tatar mosque in Poland, first mentioned in documents in 1717. Next to it lies a mizar - a distinctive Tatar cemetery close to the Polish-Belarusian border.

The community initiated the construction of a Centre for the Education and Culture of Polish Tatars and took part in a project to digitise several thousand items connected with the history and culture of the Polish Tatars.
