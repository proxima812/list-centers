This site publishes reference information about Tatar, Bashkir, Tatar-Bashkir, and Crimean Tatar cultural centers, communities, associations, and public initiatives.

It gathers information on diasporas, national societies, cultural associations, youth organizations, language courses, and educational and public projects in Russia and abroad.

## 1. Editorial policy

### The goal of the project

The core task of the project is to make finding cultural and public organizations simpler, clearer, and more accessible.

The site works as a reference catalog and makes no claim to be an official register of organizations.

### Sources of information

Cards may be compiled and updated from:

- official websites of the organizations;
- public pages on social networks;
- open online sources;
- reference material and publications;
- information published by the organizations themselves.

A separate page listing the sources we use is available here: [Sources](/en/sources).

### Publishing principles

When publishing material, the site follows these principles:

- wording is kept neutral and factual wherever possible;
- disputed or unverified information is not published as confirmed fact;
- descriptions may be shortened, edited, or standardized to keep navigation easy;
- information may be updated when more precise, more current, or fuller data appears;
- changes and clarifications may be proposed by the community through a GitHub pull request.

### Updates and corrections

Information on the site is updated by hand.

If a card is found to contain an error in the name, the region, a link, the description, or any other information, the data may be corrected after verification against the available public sources.

### Link rot

Some external links may change, move, or become unavailable over time.

When a broken link is found, the information may be updated by hand.

## 2. Privacy and technical data

### What data may be processed

The site is not built for user registration, placing orders, or creating personal accounts.

In normal use the site does not ask for a name, a phone number, an address, or any other explicitly entered personal data.

When you visit the site, the following may be processed automatically:

- standard technical data from server logs;
- information about the browser, the device, and the operating system;
- technical data required to load pages correctly;
- local interface settings saved in your own browser, such as the chosen language or theme.

### What the technical data is used for

Technical information is used only for:

- keeping the site working correctly;
- rendering the interface;
- storing local user settings;
- basic technical diagnostics and analysis of the site's stability.

### What the site does not do

The site:

- does not sell personal data;
- does not use user data for marketing of its own;
- does not require personal data in order to browse pages normally;
- does not claim to collect data that the site's functionality does not actually use.

## 3. External links

The site may contain links to third-party resources and external platforms.

Once you follow a link to an external site, the rules and policy of that resource apply, not those of this site.

The site's administration does not control the content of third-party resources reached through external links.

## 4. Content and responsibility

The information on this site is a reference, editorial, and informational resource.

The project's administration aims to keep the material accurate and current, but does not guarantee absolute completeness, permanent currency, or the absence of errors in every card and section of the site.

For anything important, we recommend verifying the information through the organizations' own official sources.
