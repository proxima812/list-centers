This site collects, structures, and refines information about Tatar, Bashkir, Tatar-Bashkir, and Crimean Tatar cultural centers, communities, national organizations, and public initiatives.

The information here is built from open sources, public material, and clarifications from members of the communities themselves.

> The information on this site is a reference and editorial resource.

## Primary sources

Work on the catalog may draw on:

- [The World Congress of Tatars](https://tatar-congress.org)
- official websites of organizations and national-cultural autonomies;
- official pages of organizations on social networks and messengers;
- open online sources and public publications;
- local corrections and additions from community representatives, members of the organizations, and users of the project.

## Secondary sources

To verify and update information we may also use:

- websites of friendship houses, cultural centers, and public associations;
- regional and municipal websites;
- publications by cultural and public projects;
- mapping and reference services, to check geography and the spelling of names;
- public pages on Instagram, Telegram, VK, and other social platforms.

## How the sources are used

- information from different sources may be cross-checked and refined;
- secondary sources are used to verify currency, links, geography, descriptions, and contacts;
- where sources disagree, we may fall back on more neutral or more cautious wording;
- some information may be edited to keep the structure of the catalog consistent and easy to navigate.

## Limitations

- This site is not an official state or legal register of organizations.
- Some information may have changed after it was published on third-party resources.
- The presence of an organization in the catalog does not confirm its current legal status.
- Some cards may hold incomplete or outdated information until they are updated.

> The presence of an organization in the catalog does not confirm its current legal status.

## Corrections and clarifications

If you notice an error, an inaccuracy, or outdated information, the following can serve as a reference for the correction:

- the official website of the organization;
- the official pages of the community;
- more recent public publications;
- confirmed clarifications from representatives of the organization or the local community.

Proposed corrections and updates can be sent through a GitHub pull request or the available feedback channels.

> Users of the project can take part in refining and updating the information through a **GitHub pull request.**
