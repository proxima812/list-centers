The site currently has two active language versions: Russian and English.

For both languages we have localized the navigation, the mobile menu, the filters, the buttons, the system labels, and the core service pages, including the home page, the list of centers, the sources, the policy, and this page about translations.

> **A localized interface does not mean every catalog entry has been translated.**

Russian remains the primary language for catalog content. Separate English translation files already exist for a share of the centers, but the coverage is not complete yet.

When a particular card has no translation, the site may show the original Russian text or a partially localized version. Language versions therefore let you use the interface in the language you prefer, but they do not guarantee a full translation of every entry.

Translation, editing, and fact-checking happen gradually. The priority is verifiable information about the centers: correct names, sources, and neutral descriptions.

> Information and translations may be updated and refined as the project develops.

If you have found an error or an inaccuracy, or you would like to help translate the material, use the feedback links available on the pages of this site.

## Available languages

- **Русский** — the primary language of the interface and the catalog content
- **English** — the interface, the service pages, and a share of the center cards

The codebase may also keep draft dictionaries for other languages, but those do not count as active language versions of the site until they are added to the list of supported locales.
